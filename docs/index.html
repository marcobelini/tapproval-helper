Claude Code asks.
Your wrist answers.

When the agent on your computer asks permission, the question lands on your Apple Watch and one tap answers it. No account, no server.

Get it on the App StoreOr try the beta

It is free, with nothing to buy. It needs an Apple Watch and Claude Code on a Mac or Linux machine. Not yet in the EU, where it follows shortly; the beta works everywhere.

How it works

Three steps, and the middle one is a sentence you say to Claude Code.

1

Install the watch app

From the App Store, on any Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later. Nothing to sign up for.

2

Tell Claude Code once

On the computer where Claude Code runs, say:

Install the Tapproval plugin from marcobelini/tapproval-helper

Claude installs its own free, open-source bridge. You approve it like anything else and never touch a terminal.

3

Raise your wrist

The watch finds your computer by itself: on your Wi-Fi, away from home through a private travel address, or through your own iCloud as a fallback.

What it does

Everything the agent asks a person for, answered where the person is.

Real questions, real options

Multiple-choice questions arrive with the same choices as on your desktop.

Sessions, live

Start a session from the wrist, follow it, send it instructions.

Your day

What was handled without you, what you decided, how much you used — and roughly how much time at the desk that saved.

The whole story

Since you installed: prompts handled, how few needed you, and every CRITICAL that was stopped for a human. No streaks, no badges, nothing to keep up.

Claude Code today

Any Mac or Linux machine running Claude Code. Other agents are on the roadmap.

Privacy

Tapproval collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours. Prompts and conversations travel between your watch and your computer, and, when the direct link is down, through your own iCloud. The bridge is open source, so you can check.

Support

The five things people ask, answered here. For anything else there is one address, and a person reads it.

The watch can't find my computer

Two causes cover nearly every case. The watch and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi, and a VPN on your iPhone must be fully connected or fully off: one that is half up hides your home network from the watch. Then open Settings → Connection on the watch. Every route gets a tick or a reason.

Pairing a watch

A first install pairs by itself. For a second watch, or after a reset, tell Claude Code on your computer:

Open Tapproval pairing

Then open Tapproval on the watch. The door stays open for half an hour.

Updating

Nothing to do. The helper checks once a day and updates itself, never while a question is waiting on your wrist. If you installed it as a plugin, Claude Code looks after it; asking it to update Tapproval in plain words is enough.

Removing it

Tell Claude Code:

Uninstall Tapproval Base

It restores your settings, keeps a backup, stops the bridge and leaves nothing running. Delete the app from the watch like any other.

My phone still shows a question I answered

The command ran. The Claude app on a phone or iPad can keep displaying a prompt the watch has already answered. It is a known Claude Code bug, not the watch, and any message sent from the phone clears it.
