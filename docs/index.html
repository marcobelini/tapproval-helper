How it works

Three steps. The middle one is a sentence you say to Claude Code, or one command for Codex.

1

Install the watch app

From the App Store, on any Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Nothing to sign up for.

2

Tell your computer once

In a Claude Code chat on your computer, say:

Install the Tapproval plugin from marcobelini/tapproval-helper

It installs the free, open-source helper. One install covers both agents. Using only Codex? Run the one-line installer in a terminal instead: Codex setup. Claude Code setup.

3

Raise your wrist

Open Tapproval on the watch while it shares your computer’s Wi-Fi. It pairs by itself; after that, a private travel address also connects you away from home.

What it does

Know which taps matter

Each request is marked with its risk, from harmless to CRITICAL. Nothing is approved for you unless you ask, and a CRITICAL never is.

Sessions, live

Follow a session as it works, send it a reply or a command, or start a new one from the wrist.

Claude Code and Codex

Both agents in one list, each session marked CC or CX, and their questions answered the same way.

Common questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Your first 100 answers are free, shared by Claude Code and Codex. Then $5 once unlocks each agent for good.

Does it work with Codex?

Yes, since version 3.0. One helper install covers Claude Code and Codex, and both show in one list on the watch, marked CC and CX.

Does it work without my iPhone?

Yes. The watch reaches your computer over the internet on its own, on Wi-Fi or cellular, through a private travel address, and on a Mac through your own iCloud.

Does it approve anything on its own?

No. Every card waits for your tap unless you choose to let the harmless tiers through, and a CRITICAL request never goes through on its own.

Support

The watch can't find my computer

Put both on the same Wi-Fi, and make sure a VPN on your iPhone is fully on or fully off. Then open Settings on the watch and tap the status card: every route gets a tick or a reason.

Pairing another watch

Tell Claude Code:

Open Tapproval pairing

Then open Tapproval on the watch within ten minutes.

Cards arrive, but sessions don’t

“Approvals only” means iCloud is carrying your cards. Sessions need a direct link: join your computer’s Wi-Fi or check the away address in Connection.

Updating, removing and the rest: the setup guide. Anything else: tapproval@thoughtfulsteward.org, read by a person.
